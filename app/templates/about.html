About PriceOrbit

PriceOrbit is a grocery price tracking and prediction system that monitors tariff data, supply chain signals, and real-time retail prices to help shoppers make informed buying decisions.

Features

  • Live Kroger price integration
  • Price history charts and trend analysis
  • Tariff-aware health scoring per product
  • Custom price drop alerts
  • Store locator with nearby search
